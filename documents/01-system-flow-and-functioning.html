1. System Overview & Objectives

Monit Paper Agency ERP is a fully integrated enterprise resource planning system built for Monit Paper Agency, a paper trading company based in Indore, Madhya Pradesh. The system digitizes and centralizes the entire paper trading lifecycle — from customer inquiry capture through procurement from mills, quality-controlled warehouse receipt, FIFO-based inventory management, dispatch planning, challan generation, in-transit tracking, and invoicing.

1.1 Business Objectives

  • Centralize all customer inquiries and sales orders with end-to-end status tracking
  • Provide real-time inventory visibility across multiple warehouses using FIFO allocation logic
  • Automate coverage analysis — instantly identify stock surplus or shortage against confirmed SO requirements
  • Streamline procurement from mills with PO issuance, mill confirmation tracking, and GRN-based receipt
  • Manage pick planning, truck loading, challan generation, and in-transit monitoring
  • Enable role-based access so each team sees a tailored view relevant to their function
  • Provide management with consolidated dashboards and reports across sales, operations, and finance

3. User Roles & Access Control

The system enforces four distinct roles. Each role is assigned a tailored navigation sidebar, filtered data views, and scoped action permissions. Role enforcement is applied at the UI layer (Phase 1) and will extend to the API layer in Phase 2.

1 Owner / Admin Full Access
  • Full access to all 30+ pages and modules
  • All four dashboards (Owner, Accounts, Salesman, Planner)
  • Create, edit, approve, and delete any record
  • Manage masters: materials, mills, customers, rates, etc.
  • User and role management
  • Full visibility into all financial and operational reports
2 Salesman Sales Access
  • Create and manage inquiries and sales orders
  • View all inquiries and SOs (not filtered to self)
  • Coverage: view only (cannot allocate)
  • Salesman Dashboard: own performance metrics
  • Customer Master: view only
  • No access to procurement, logistics, or finance modules
3 Planner Operations Access
  • Purchase Orders: create and manage
  • Mill Tracker, GRN, Stock Lots
  • Dispatch Queue, Pick Plan, Truck Load Plan
  • Challan & Loading, In-Transit Tracking
  • Planner Dashboard
  • No access to financial data or master management
4 Customer Portal Access
  • Customer self-service portal (minimal sidebar)
  • View and submit only their own inquiries
  • Cannot edit inquiries once past Draft status
  • No access to any internal operational pages
  • Data strictly isolated — cannot see other customers
Role Enforcement: Data filtering is applied at the Context layer. Customers receive only inquiries where inquiry.customer === user.customerName. The Sidebar navigation itself changes based on role, so unauthorized routes are not even visible to limited roles.

4. Module Overview — Sales Pipeline

Customer Inquiry
/inquiry

The entry point of every sales cycle. Captures all inbound paper requirements from customers received via phone, WhatsApp, email, or in-person visit. Each inquiry can contain multiple material requirements (different paper types, GSM, size, delivery location) and is assigned to a salesman.

AttributeDetail
Key ActionsCreate new inquiry, Edit (role-based), View detail, Convert to Sales Order
Filters AvailableStatus, Priority, Source, Customer, Date Range, Salesman
KPIs ShownTotal Inquiries · Confirmed · In Progress · Rejected / Lost
Feeds IntoSales Order (upon confirmation)
Customer ViewCustomers see only their own inquiries; can create new; cannot edit after submission

STATUS FLOW:

Draft→ Stock Checked→ Waiting Mill→ Mill Confirmed→ Customer Confirmed→ Confirmed→ Converted
Terminal: Rejected Lost On Hold
Roles: Admin / Salesman / CustomerContext: InquiryContextPriority: Low / Medium / High / Urgent
Sales Order
/orders

Represents the formal agreement to supply paper to a customer. Created either from a confirmed inquiry (auto-fills customer, material, qty) or manually. Each SO has line items defining material, ordered quantity, agreed rate, and delivery schedule. The SO is the central anchor record connecting procurement and dispatch.

AttributeDetail
Key ActionsCreate SO, Convert from Inquiry (pre-fills all data), Edit, View detail
Filters AvailableStatus, Customer, Salesman, Date Range, Value Range
KPIs ShownTotal SOs · Confirmed Pending PO · Dispatched · Total Order Value
Feeds IntoCoverage Analysis (requirements), Purchase Order (Against-SO type)
Draft→ Confirmed→ Partially Dispatched→ Dispatched→ Closed   Cancelled
Roles: Admin / SalesmanContext: SalesOrderContextLinks: Inquiry (source), linkedPoIds
Coverage Analysis
/coverage

The strategic decision bridge between sales demand and supply. Compares confirmed SO material requirements against available physical stock (Stock Lots) plus in-transit stock (confirmed POs not yet received). Clearly shows surplus or shortage per material, enabling planners to either allocate existing stock or raise a new Purchase Order.

Coverage FormulaDescription
Coverage = Physical Stock + In-Transit − SO RequirementPositive = surplus; Negative = shortage → raise PO
Roles: Admin / PlannerReads: StockContext.getAvailableByMaterial()Action: Allocate lot OR Create PO

5. Module Overview — Procurement Pipeline

Purchase Order
/purchase-orders

Issued to paper mills to procure stock. Supports two distinct modes: For Stock (buffer inventory, items added manually) and Against Sales Order (demand-driven, material lines auto-populate from the linked SO). The form dynamically adapts its fields based on the selected PO type.

PO TypeBehaviour & Fields
For StockDelivery to Monit's own godown address · No customer/SO section · No blind shipment option · Manual item entry with "Add Item" button
Against Sales OrderLinks to specific SO · All SO line items auto-fill immediately · Blind shipment compact toggle shown · Customer delivery address shown · When mill selected, purchase rates auto-load from Rate Master per material
Draft→ Sent to Mill→ Mill Confirmed→ Partially Received→ Fully Received→ Closed
Roles: Admin / PlannerContext: SalesOrderContext (reads pendingPoSOs)Auto-fill: buildItemsFromSO() + Rate Master lookup
Mill Order Tracker
/mill-tracker

Post-PO issuance, this module tracks each order at the mill. Records mill acknowledgment, production start, expected dispatch date, and actual dispatch date along with the LR (Lorry Receipt) number. Provides early visibility into potential delays.

StepAction
1. PO SentPO transmitted to mill (manual or via system)
2. AcknowledgedMill confirms receipt and provides expected dates
3. Under ProductionPaper being manufactured / reel cutting in progress
4. Dispatched from MillVehicle departed mill; LR number attached
Roles: Admin / PlannerFeeds: GRN (expected arrival date, LR number)
GRN — Goods Receipt Note
/grn

Records the physical arrival of paper at the godown. The workflow includes unloading verification, quality inspection, bin assignment, and the critical final step of moving material into live inventory.

StageAction PerformedSystem Effect
DraftVehicle arrives; unloading begins; received qty recordedGRN record created in StockContext
QC PendingMaterial submitted for quality checkStockContext.updateGRN() — status updated
ApprovedQC Manager records condition, quality grade, damages, shortagesQC fields saved; discrepancy flagged if qty mismatch
Stock UpdatedPlanner confirms bin location; moves to inventoryStockContext.addToStock(grn) — creates new StockLot
Roles: Admin / PlannerContext: StockContext (updateGRN, addToStock)Key Trigger: addToStock() creates a live StockLot
Stock Lots
/stock-lots

The live inventory ledger. Every approved GRN produces exactly one Stock Lot entry. Each lot tracks four quantity states and the physical bin where the paper resides. The FIFO rule ensures the oldest received stock is always consumed first.

Quantity FieldMeaning
Received QtyOriginal quantity received in this lot (immutable after creation)
Current QtyPhysical quantity remaining after dispatches
Allocated QtyQuantity reserved against confirmed SOs (soft reservation)
Available QtyCurrent Qty − Allocated Qty = freely allocatable stock
Available→ Allocated→ Depleted
Roles: Admin / Planner / Salesman (view)Context: StockContextFIFO: Sorted by receivedDate ascending

6. Module Overview — Logistics & Dispatch Pipeline

Dispatch Queue
/dispatch-queue

A prioritized work queue of all confirmed SO line items ready for dispatch. Shows material, customer, required quantity, allocated stock lots, and urgency indicators. Planners use this to initiate pick planning for selected SO lines.

Entry ConditionSO status = Confirmed AND stock allocated from at least one StockLot
Key ActionsView queue, re-prioritize lines, select items to create Pick Plan
Pick Plan (FIFO + Bin)
/pick-plan

Generates the warehouse picking instruction. Uses FIFO (First In, First Out) ordering of stock lots combined with bin location data to direct the warehouse team to exactly which physical bin to pick from, and in what quantity, for each delivery. Manual lot override is supported for exceptions.

AttributeDetail
FIFO AlgorithmPrimary sort: receivedDate ascending (oldest first) · Secondary sort: binLocation
System EffectStockContext.allocateLot() reserves qty on selected lots
Feeds IntoTruck Load Plan (approved pick items grouped into truck loads)
Truck Load Planner
/truck-load-plan

Groups multiple SO deliveries into a single truck load to maximize transport efficiency. Supports drag-and-drop item arrangement per truck, gross weight and volume monitoring, and transporter/vehicle assignment. One confirmed truck load generates one Challan.

Key ActionsCreate truck load, drag picked items onto truck, assign transporter, set vehicle, confirm load
Challan & Loading
/challan

The official delivery document generated from a confirmed truck load. Records itemized loaded materials, vehicle and driver details, delivery address, and customer signature on receipt. Acts as the legal dispatch record and triggers permanent stock reduction.

System EffectStockContext.dispatchLot() — permanently reduces currentQty in allocated lots
Links ToIn-Transit (created on dispatch), Sales Invoice (on delivery confirmation)
Draft→ Generated→ Dispatched→ Delivered
In-Transit Tracking
/in-transit

Monitors all dispatched challans not yet delivered. Provides real-time visibility into material in motion: vehicle details, estimated arrival, transporter contact, and delivery confirmation. Updates the corresponding SO lines to "Partially Dispatched" or "Dispatched" status on delivery.

7. Module Overview — Finance & Dashboards

Purchase Invoices
/purchase-invoices

Tracks mill invoices against received GRNs. Performs three-way matching: PO ↔ GRN ↔ Invoice. Records invoice amount, GST breakup, payment status, and due dates for payables management.

Sales Invoices
/sales-invoices

Customer-facing GST invoices generated upon delivery confirmation. Linked to Challans and SO lines. Manages outstanding balances, payment recording, and receivables ageing for the accounts team.

Reports (4 Categories)
